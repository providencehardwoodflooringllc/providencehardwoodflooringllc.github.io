Craft you notice by living with it.

Providence Hardwood Flooring LLC is a locally owned, family-operated flooring company based in Salisbury, North Carolina. The brothers behind the company stay hands-on from the first conversation through the final walkthrough.

Approach

A floor is one of the largest visual surfaces in a home. It should feel quiet, precise and resolved.

The approach is straightforward: prepare carefully, communicate clearly, execute cleanly and leave the room looking intentional. Installation, refinishing, repair and restoration all start with the same idea — the floor should support the room instead of fighting it.
